What is the app?

The two scenarios under test. 1 is the app as EAI Setup: the six steps the real installer runs, ending in a choice of AI app. 2 is the app as the coding agent: many of the same steps, but you finish in a chat rather than in Copilot. The third card is the spike — how much of the setup can stop being the user's problem if 2 is where we land.

1. App as EAI Setup

The six steps, then pick your AI app. GitHub Copilot opens on the new project and you build there.

  • Install, prerequisites, sign in, name it
  • Ends at the /eai prompt in Copilot
  • Previews open in Safari
  • The EAI app's job is done
Run scenario 1 →

2. App as a coding agent

The same steps, but the app opens a chat instead of another app. Building happens right there.

  • Identical to 1 until you're signed in
  • Previews open beside the chat
  • “Open in…” moves the session to your editor
  • Moving is offered, never required
Run scenario 2 →

2b. The spike: hide the setup

Setup only. Install, sign in, and the setup happens in the chat before you type anything. Stops at “ready”.

  • One screen before the chat: sign in
  • “Set it up for me” — or choose it yourself
  • Nothing installed on the Mac at all…
  • …until you ask for your own editor
