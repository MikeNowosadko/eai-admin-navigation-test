Setup form vs harness

Two things vary: which form does the setup, and whose harness you build in afterwards. The third drops the form altogether and lets the harness do the setup itself.

EAI Setup form and external harness

The six-step form, then pick your AI app. GitHub Copilot opens on the new project and you build there.

  • Install, prerequisites, sign in, name it
  • Ends at the /eai prompt in Copilot
  • Previews open in Safari
  • The EAI app's job is done

EAI Setup form and EAI harness

The same form, but the app opens its own chat instead of another app. Building happens right there.

  • Identical to the first until you're signed in
  • Previews open beside the chat
  • “Open in…” moves the session to your editor
  • Moving is offered, never required
